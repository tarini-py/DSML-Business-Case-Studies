# Yulu Micro-Mobility Demand Analysis

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Yulu** is India’s leading micro-mobility service provider, offering shared electric cycles for daily commuting. This project analyzes factors affecting the demand for these cycles and provides actionable insights for improving operations, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Understand which factors influence bicycle rental demand.
- Identify key trends in user behavior (registered vs casual users).
- Provide recommendations for operations, marketing, and fleet management.
- Suggest measurable **KPIs** for tracking performance.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

**1. Daily & Weekly Trends**  
- Rentals are stable across weekdays and weekends; day type has minimal impact.  
- Peak hours: **8–9 AM** and **5–7 PM** — aligned with commuting patterns.  

**2. Weather Effects**  
| Weather Type | % of Rentals | Demand Level |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| Clear / Partly Cloudy | 66% | High |
| Mist / Cloudy | 26% | Moderate |
| Rain / Snow | 8% | Low |

**3. Seasonal Trends**  
- Winter, Summer, and Fall show slightly higher rentals.  
- Spring shows marginally lower rentals.  

**4. Temperature & Comfort**  
- Most rentals occur between **15°C–30°C**.  
- Rentals decline above 30°C due to heat discomfort.  

**5. User Distribution**  
- Majority of rentals are by **registered users**.  
- Casual users contribute fewer rentals, mostly for occasional trips.  

---

## 💡 Recommendations

**Operational Planning**  
- Maintain consistent bike availability and staffing across all days.  
- Adjust fleet allocation based on weather forecasts.  

**Seasonal Strategy**  
- Slightly higher attention to Winter, Summer, and Fall.  
- Plan maintenance schedules during low-demand periods.  

**Marketing & Engagement**  
- Run promotions during low-demand weather or seasonal dips.  
- Encourage casual users to convert to registered users.  

**Data-Driven Monitoring**  
- Continuously track rentals by hour, weather, and season.  
- Use insights to optimize fleet utilization and reduce operational costs.  

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Daily Rentals:** Total, Casual vs Registered  
- **Rentals by Weather Condition**  
- **Rentals by Temperature Range**  
- **Hourly Peak Utilization**  
- **Monthly & Seasonal Demand Trends**  
- **Registered vs Casual User Ratio**  
- **Fleet Availability vs Usage Efficiency**  
- **Effectiveness of Promotions & Incentives**  

---

## 📝 Conclusion

By leveraging weather-aware operations, seasonal adjustments, and engaging registered users, **Yulu** can:  
- Optimize operational efficiency  
- Improve customer satisfaction  
- Increase overall revenue  

---

## 📂 Data Source

- Historical rental data (2011–2012) including temperature, humidity, windspeed, weather, and user type (casual/registered).
